This notice explains how AImpetus Ltd ("AImpetus", "we", "us") collects and uses personal
information when you visit aimpetus.ai or contact us.

Who we are
AImpetus Ltd is the controller of your personal information. We are a company registered
in England and Wales, company number 17574177, with our registered office at 68
Oatlands Road, Shinfield, RG2 9DN. We are registered with the Information
Commissioner's Office (ICO), registration number [Placeholder: ICO registration
number].
If you have any questions about this notice or your information, email
contact@aimpetus.ai.

What information we collect
When you contact us: your name, work email address, organisation, the type of
organisation you represent, and anything you include in your message.
When you book a conversation: your name, email address, the time you choose and
any notes you add. Bookings are handled by [Placeholder: booking tool, for example
Microsoft Bookings or Calendly].
When we work with you or your organisation: business contact details and
correspondence needed to discuss, deliver and invoice our services.
When you visit the website: basic technical information our hosting provider needs
to run and secure the site, such as your IP address and browser type. See our cookie
notice for more.
We don't knowingly collect special category information (such as health information)
through the website. Please don't include it in your message.

Your rights
You have the right to:
ask for a copy of your information
ask us to correct it
ask us to delete it
ask us to restrict how we use it
object to us using it, including for any marketing
ask us to transfer it to you or someone else
withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent
To use any of these rights, email contact@aimpetus.ai. We'll reply within one month.

Complaints
If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first so we can
put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office
at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Changes to this notice
We may update this notice from time to time. The latest version will always be on this

Cookie notice
Last updated: [Placeholder: publication date]
Cookies are small files a website stores on your device. Some are essential to make a site
work. Others, such as analytics cookies, are optional.

Cookies we use
We keep cookies to a minimum. At launch, aimpetus.ai uses only strictly necessary
cookies, which the site needs to work securely. These don't need your consent.

Accessibility statement
Last updated: [Placeholder: publication date]
We want everyone to be able to use aimpetus.ai, including people who use assistive
technology. Many of the organisations we work with have to meet public-sector
accessibility standards, and we hold our own website to the same standard.

Our commitment
We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. That
means you should be able to:
navigate the whole site using just a keyboard
use the site with a screen reader
zoom in up to 400% without text spilling off the screen
change colours, contrast and fonts using your browser or device settings
read text that meets AA colour contrast

Checklist before publishing
Pay the ICO data protection fee and add the registration number to the privacy notice.
A micro business (turnover up to £632,000 or no more than 10 staff) pays the tier 1 fee
of £52 a year, or £47 by direct debit (ICO guidance).
Name the hosting provider, email software and booking tool in the privacy notice.
Fill in the cookie table once the site is built, or remove the rows that don't apply.
Don't add analytics until a consent banner is in place.
Test accessibility and fill in the test date and results.
Add the publication date to all three pages.
Link all three pages from the website footer, and the privacy notice from the contact
form.
Make sure contact@aimpetus.ai is set up and monitored.
