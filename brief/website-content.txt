This is the copy for every page of the launch site, in page order and ready to paste. It
follows the agreed brand foundation: data and digital services with AI at the core, the
tagline "AI in the engine room.", and copy written for buyers, with NHS and public-sector
experience as proof.
Conventions
H1, H2, H3 mark heading levels. Use one H1 per page.
[Button: …] is a button and [Link: …] is a text link, with the destination page in
brackets.
SEO title (under 60 characters) and Meta description (under 155 characters) sit at
the top of each page.
[Placeholder: …] marks something to fill in before go-live. All of them are listed in the
last section.
Nothing here claims AImpetus clients, partners, framework places or accreditations.
Founder experience is always described as the founders' own.

Developer brief: build requirements
This section is for whoever builds the site. Use the copy in this document exactly as
written, and the brand kit in the AImpetus Brand folder.

Scope
Pages: Home, Services overview, four service pages (Data and platforms, AI to benefit,
Digital transformation, Delivery assurance), How we work, About us, Insights,
Contact, Privacy notice, Cookie notice, Accessibility statement.
Not in scope at launch: case studies, blog CMS beyond simple article pages, client
login, analytics.
Domain: aimpetus.ai, served over HTTPS, with www redirecting to the bare domain
(or the other way round, consistently).

Brand
Logo: use the SVG files from the brand kit (primary on light, reversed on dark). Never
retype the logo as live text.
Colours: Ink #0E2A2F, Teal #0F766E, Mint #5EEAD4 (on dark only), Amber #F59E0B
(graphics only, never text on white), Paper #F7F7F4, secondary text #4A5A5D.
Fonts: Space Grotesk Bold for headings, Inter for body text (both free Google Fonts).
"AI" in headings: in Space Grotesk the capital I looks like a lowercase l, so "AI" can read
as "Al". Wherever "AI" appears in a heading, give the I small serifs to match the logo, or
set those two letters in a font with a serifed I. Check every heading that contains "AI".
Imagery: no stock photos of robots, glowing brains or server rooms. Use the chevron
motif, simple diagrams and, later, real photos of the founders.

H2: Why we started AImpetus
We've seen too many promising data and AI ideas stall before they reached a live service.
The technology usually works. What's missing is the groundwork: reliable data, modern
platforms, sound governance and steady delivery. AImpetus exists to put that
groundwork in place, so AI can do real work in the engine room of public services.
The name says what we're for: AI, and the impetus to move good ideas into everyday use.
H2: Why now
AI has moved from experiment to expectation. But the organisations that gain most won't
be the ones with the most technology. They'll be the ones that stay focused on outcomes.
That's why we started AImpetus now.
Outcomes, not hype. The real test is benefit delivered: better services, lower costs
and measurable impact. Technology is the means, not the goal.
Understanding the business matters as much as the technology. AI and digital tools
move fast. Knowing how an organisation really works, its people, pressures and
priorities, matters just as much.
The groundwork is the gap. The tools are ready. What often isn't ready is the data, the
platforms and the governance underneath. That's exactly where we work.
We both stepped out of conventional IT services to see the work from a different angle:
new technology, new ways of delivering services, and the ground reality of making change
stick. AImpetus brings that experience together, so the organisations we work with get
the benefit, not the hype.
