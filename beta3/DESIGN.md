# Beta 3 – light, people-first

Served at `/beta3/`. Started as a copy of beta1 (content, data, accessibility patterns, form logic). Status: Home page built and awaiting review. The other pages still use beta1's dark layouts and will be rebuilt in this style after review.

## Direction (changed by the founders, 13 Nov 2026)
The first Beta 3 ("Engine room: Schematic", dark with line diagrams) was rejected as "childish, like an architecture diagram, not a professional website". The founders asked for a **white or light background with dark teal** instead, and gave thapar.tech as a reference: a plain, conventional business site with a big photo of people working, white throughout, one dark colour and a dark footer. This replaces the dark-first Engine room brief for this beta only.

How it differs from the other two:
- **Beta 1:** dark, editorial, illustrated.
- **Beta 2:** structured grids, a dark hero and a bold split-band stroke.
- **Beta 3:** white, calm and photographic, with almost no decoration.

## Key decisions
- **Light theme** (`.theme-light` on `<body>` via `BaseLayout light`): ink text, slate secondary text, teal links and buttons, pill-shaped buttons (`--radius: 2rem`). It's opt-in per page until every page is rebuilt, and then becomes the default.
- **Colour budget:** white and paper bands; teal for accents and key numbers; one dark-teal block (the CTA panel, `.on-ink`) plus the dark footer. Mint appears only as soft tints and on dark, because it fails contrast on white.
- **Photography:** AI-generated by the founders (`assets/photos/generated/`, originals unchanged). `scripts/prepare-photos.mjs` trims the hero, slightly desaturates and outputs JPEGs to `src/assets/photos/`; Astro serves responsive AVIF/WebP. Hero = team at a table; How we work = whiteboard workshop; Contact = one-to-one conversation (planned). Alt text is empty because the photos are decorative stock-style images, not information.
- **Founder photos:** natural colour with the beta1 grade (`scripts/prepare-founder-photos.mjs`), shown without the chevron badge.
- **Home sections:**
  - the hero (text plus a rounded photo with a soft mint glow);
  - a key-facts band (60 / 10+ / 4);
  - the problem as a two-column statement;
  - "Senior people. Proven delivery." as a headed two-column list with icon circles and hairlines (no boxes);
  - the services as white cards with "Explore →";
  - How we work as a photo and three numbered steps;
  - the founders as simple cards;
  - the CTA as a rounded dark-teal panel.
- **Header:** white, primary logo, plain links, teal pill button. On phones the menu is a full-screen light overlay with focus kept inside it, Escape to close and page scrolling locked.

## Contrast (on white unless stated)
Ink 15.1:1 · slate 7.2:1 · teal 5.5:1 · teal-dark 7.9:1 · white on teal button 5.5:1 · ink on mint button (CTA) 10.2:1 · slate on paper 6.7:1 · teal-dark on paper 7.3:1.
