# Beta 3 – light, people-first

Served at `/beta3/`. Started as a copy of beta1 (content, data, accessibility patterns, form logic). Status: all 15 pages built in the light style and checked.

## Direction (changed by the founders, 13 Nov 2026)
The first Beta 3 ("Engine room: Schematic", dark with line diagrams) was rejected as "childish, like an architecture diagram, not a professional website". The founders asked for a **white or light background with dark teal** instead, and gave thapar.tech as a reference: a plain, conventional business site with a big photo of people working, white throughout, one dark colour and a dark footer. This replaces the dark-first Engine room brief for this beta only.

How it differs from the other two:
- **Beta 1:** dark, editorial, illustrated.
- **Beta 2:** structured grids, a dark hero and a bold split-band stroke.
- **Beta 3:** white, calm and photographic, with almost no decoration.

## Key decisions
- **Light theme** (`.theme-light` on `<body>`, the `BaseLayout` default): ink text, slate secondary text, teal links and buttons, pill-shaped buttons (`--radius: 2rem`).
- **Colour budget:** white and paper bands; teal for accents and key numbers; one dark-teal block (the CTA panel, `.on-ink`) plus the dark footer. Mint appears only as soft tints and on dark, because it fails contrast on white.
- **Photography:** AI-generated by the founders (`assets/photos/generated/`, originals unchanged). `scripts/prepare-photos.mjs` trims the hero, slightly desaturates and outputs JPEGs to `src/assets/photos/`; Astro serves responsive AVIF/WebP. Hero = team at a table; How we work = whiteboard workshop; Contact = one-to-one conversation (planned). Alt text is empty because the photos are decorative stock-style images, not information.
- **Logo stripes** (`LogoStripes.astro`, revised after two light reviews): the mark's chevrons as **thick** stripes cutting across the whole section, top edge to bottom edge, behind the photo. The point sits near the top edge, so only a sliver (about 6%) of the upper arm shows, and the long lower arm sweeps down to the bottom edge.
  - **Hero:** amber and mint pair, anchored right, partly off the page edge.
  - **How we work:** one pale mint stripe, its point just beyond the photo's right edge.
  - **Clearance:** measured clear of all text at 1024–1920 px (at least 57 px).
  - **Phones:** stripes are limited to the photo area.
- **Spacing:** sections are tighter in beta3 (`--section-space: clamp(3rem, 2rem + 3.5vw, 5.5rem)`; hero `clamp(2rem, 1rem + 3vw, 3.75rem)`) after the "too much blank space" review.
- **Founder photos:** natural colour with the beta1 grade (`scripts/prepare-founder-photos.mjs`), shown without the chevron badge.
- **Home sections:**
  - the hero (text plus a rounded photo with a soft mint glow);
  - a key-facts band (60 / 10+ / 4);
  - the problem as a two-column statement;
  - "Senior people. Proven delivery." as a headed two-column list with icon circles and hairlines (no boxes);
  - the services as white cards with "Explore →";
  - How we work as a photo and three numbered steps;
  - the founders as simple cards;
  - the CTA as a rounded dark-teal panel.
- **Header:** white, primary logo, plain links, teal pill button. On phones the menu is a full-screen light overlay with focus kept inside it, Escape to close and page scrolling locked.

- **Shared components:** `PageHeader` (light header with breadcrumb; with a photo it's two columns with pale stripes behind the photo, and without one the pale stripes sit on the right on wide screens only), `PhotoFrame`, `ReasonList` (headed list with icon circles and hairlines), `ServiceCards`, `NumberedSteps`, `CtaPanel` (rounded dark-teal panel).
- **Interior pages:**
  - Services: overview in a mint panel, then cards.
  - Service pages:
    - "Sound familiar?" (warm amber panel) beside "What you get" (mint panel with ticks);
    - "What we do" as a headed list;
    - the experience as a large statement with an amber rule;
    - "Other services" cards and the CTA.
  - How we work: workshop photo in the header, big numbered steps, a headed list, and "Ways to work with us" as three top-ruled cards.
  - About: story with a name callout, Why now, large founder profiles in natural colour, beliefs.
  - Contact: conversation photo in the header, a dark booking strip, then the form and other ways to reach us.
  - Legal pages: light header with a readable column.
- **Stripe rules** (after the founders' review):
  - amber and mint are always the **same thickness**;
  - interior headers use one fixed stripe size (40rem tall) anchored to the header's top-right, and every header is 39rem tall from 1200 px, so the stripes look **static** when switching pages;
  - header stripes show from 1200 px wide (pushed further off-edge up to 1440 px) and are measured at least 18 px clear of the text;
  - the problem panel's stripe piece enters through the top border and leaves through the right border, so it is never cut mid-panel.

- **Header cards** (after "Services, About and Insights look empty; no more people photos"): a white card in the photo slot, sitting on the same stripes (`HeaderCard.astro`, `PageHeader` `media` slot):
  - **Services:** a 2×2 grid of service tiles that link to each service (one per row on the narrowest phones).
  - **About:** a facts-only card (Founded 2026 · 60 years of combined experience · 2 founders · 10+ years on NHS national programmes).
  - **Insights:** a decorative stack of empty article cards with the mark.
- **Our approach** (How we work): three white stage cards on a mint band, joined by teal chevron connectors (right on wide screens, down on phones). Each has a large number and an icon, and "Scale or stop" ends with a two-tone Scale / Stop split.
